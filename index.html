About the show

Harbor Frequency is a class-project landing page for a podcast that does not exist yet. The harbor, the guests, and the host voice are fictional on purpose: this assignment is about a transparent AI workflow, not a real news brand.

DISCLAIMER: Harbor Frequency is a fictional podcast promo created for a web-design assignment. No real guests, brands, or listeners are represented here.

REFLECTIONS: AI helped me come up with the idea for this mini‑project, and it handled many parts I wasn’t confident about. A lot of the tools and elements were things I haven’t used in a long time, so AI filled in most of the gaps for me. Even though it saved time in some areas, it also slowed me down. I spent a lot of time fixing mistakes, re‑doing sections, and trying to understand code that I wasn’t familiar with. Working with audio in HTML was especially frustrating because it’s not something I normally do, and I had to stop several times to figure out what went wrong. Next time, I would look up more tutorials and examples before starting, so I’m not relying on AI for every step. My biggest ethical concern is that AI can produce answers that look correct even when they aren’t, and it’s easy to trust it without fully understanding the results
